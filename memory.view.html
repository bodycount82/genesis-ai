What reconstructive means here
Reinforcement

Importance moves 10% of the remaining distance toward 8.5. Ambient per-step and Mission reads do not reinforce.

Reconsolidation

A top recall can become labile for ~10 minutes. Similar facts may overwrite at lower thresholds; episode mood retints 15% toward the present.

04 · Consolidation

At rest, experience
becomes knowledge.

Related episodes draw together. The strongest witness stays. Repetition becomes a rule. The rest can fold away. Choose how deeply Genesis sleeps.

Replay rank

importance + 2× reinforcement + 2× |emotion|. Top eight sampled traces strengthen.

Clustering

Cosine ≥ 0.55 or two shared tokens. Two or more related episodes may distill into a semantic rule.

Folding

The strongest witness survives; weaker siblings fold unless flashbulb-protected.

Serendipity

Occasionally two distant episodes cross-link into a low-importance dream seed.

Reveal the clocks and limits
Episodic

weight = 0.33 ^ effective days. Powered-off time costs nothing; strong, emotional traces can age ~3.5× slower.

Semantic / procedural

Wall-clock horizons: ~60d × (1+r) for weak knowledge; ~180d × (1+r/2) for weak procedures.

Flashbulb

Reinforcement ≥ 5 gives graded protection during decay and consolidation—not permanence.

Capacity

The defaults are 4,000 episodic / 1,500 semantic / 400 procedural records, with lowest-retention pruning first. Capacity and decay horizons are adjustable in Settings. Indexed retrieval has been exercised at 500,000 episodic and 500,000 semantic entries without noticeable search slowdown; there is no fixed architectural ceiling beyond configured storage and hardware.
